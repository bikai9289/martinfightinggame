Beginner guide

How to Play No Filter Fighters

A one-on-one 2D fighter with a sidekick assist. If you have played any fighting game before, the structure will be familiar; if you have not, this page covers what you need for your first hour.

Step 1: Get the game running

  1. Use a desktop browser if you can — Chrome, Edge or Firefox, with a real keyboard.
  2. Open the game from the creator's itch.io page (see where to play). The first load takes a while because the build is large.
  3. Do not switch tabs while it loads — the browser may pause the tab and stall the download.
  4. On a phone or tablet, turn the screen sideways. Touch buttons appear on their own.

Step 4: The habits that matter

Pick a fighter and a different sidekick

Every match uses two names on your side. The same character cannot be both. If you only remember one face from the show, make that the fighter and pick anyone else as the assist.

Block first, attack second

In every fighting game, the player who blocks well beats the player who attacks fast. Holding back to block costs nothing; eating a heavy hit costs a chunk of your health bar. Spend your first few matches mostly blocking and watching what your opponent does.

Stop jumping

New players jump constantly, and jumping is the most punishable thing you can do — you are committed, in the air, and unable to block. Walk forward instead. You will lose fewer rounds immediately.

Learn one character, not all eleven

Character knowledge matters more than roster breadth. Pick one fighter — Martin Payne is the straightforward choice — and play twenty matches with them before switching. You will learn the move timing, which is the actual skill.

Common problems

The game does not load

Almost always a tab that was backgrounded during the download. Close it, reopen the game, and leave the tab focused until the title screen appears.

Online matches will not connect

Online Versus uses a room code. You need the other person online at the same time. It is not a large matchmaking pool.

The game stutters

Close other tabs and anything else using the GPU. Browser fighters are heavier than they look. On the PC zip, the creator includes a compatibility-mode launcher if the window opens black.

Windows blocked the .exe

The downloadable build is an unsigned indie zip. The creator's instructions are: More info, then Run anyway. Only do that for the file you got from the itch.io page, not from a random host.
