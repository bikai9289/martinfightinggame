No Filter Fighters Characters — Full Roster

No Filter Fighters currently has eleven playable characters. Each fighter can also be chosen as a sidekick; a name can only appear once in a match. Most of the roster is taken from the cast of the Martin TV series rather than invented for the game.

Roster and stage names follow the creator's itch.io listing for v13.2 (16 October 2026). If a name differs from what you see in game, the in-game screen is correct.

The main cast

Martin Payne

The show's lead and the obvious starting character. The creator listing names a cartoon Martin Mic Drop among his supers. If you have never played the game before, start here and pick a sidekick you already know from the series.

Gina Waters

Martin's partner and the second half of the show's central pairing. Use her if you want a familiar face from the apartment scenes rather than one of the later joke characters.

Pam James

Gina's best friend, and the character whose verbal sparring with Martin drove a large share of the show's comedy. One of her named supers on the creator page is Party Over Here.

Cole Brown

The laid-back member of the group. The creator listing calls out Big Shirley as a signature super — the kind of joke move that only lands if you already watched the show.

Tommy Strawn

Martin's best friend, and — for anyone who watched the show — the source of its longest-running job joke. Job Roulette is one of the supers named on the game page.

Sheneneh Jenkins

Martin's neighbour, one of the show's most quoted characters, and the roster's most distinctive design. If you are coming to the game because of a clip, there is a good chance this is the fighter you saw.

Recurring roles and later additions

Varnell Hill

Martin's talk-show host persona from the series. Fights on The Varnell Hill Show stage; the creator listing names Catfish Cannon as a signature super.

Dragonfly Jones

Martin's martial-arts persona, complete with the assistant Kenji. His stage is Dragonfly's Dojo. The page calls out the “Kenji, ATTACK!” super.

Jerome

The smooth-talking ice man from the show. The creator listing highlights freeze combos as his identity in the fight, which is why he plays differently from the rest of the sitcom roster.

Bruh-Man

The fifth-floor neighbour from the series, added as a playable fighter in v12.9. His stage is The Fifth Flo'. Named moves on the listing include Sammich Toss and FROM THE FIFTH FLO'.

Bob from Marketing

The newest fighter, added in v13.2. He throws pizza boxes, builds Hype by dancing, and has a super that starts when he answers the phone. He also brings The Corporate Party stage. Treat him as a current-build addition rather than a core sitcom regular.

Stages

The current listing ships with nine stages, each modelled on a location from the series or a later joke set:

  • The Varnell Hill Show
  • Martin's Apartment
  • Club GTD
  • Sheneneh's Salon
  • Nipsey's
  • Dragonfly's Dojo
  • The Playa's Ball
  • The Fifth Flo'
  • The Corporate Party

Every stage has its own theme music. Stage choice is not just wallpaper: the creator describes wall, ceiling and floor breaks that can move the fight into another arena.

Which character should you pick first?

  • Brand new to fighting games: Martin Payne — the lead, and the character the rest of the roster is written around.
  • Here for a specific joke: Sheneneh Jenkins, Tommy Strawn or Bruh-Man — pick the face you actually remember from the show.
  • Want the newest kit: Bob from Marketing — added in the current version, with a Hype meter of his own.
  • Want a different rhythm: Jerome — freeze combos, not a standard punch-kick sitcom fighter.

Whatever you pick as the fighter, pick a different name as the sidekick. The same character cannot occupy both slots.
